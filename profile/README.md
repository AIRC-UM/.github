## Welcome to the AIRC Lab！

Welcome to the GitHub homepage of the Artificial Intelligence and Robot Control (AIRC) Lab. 

We are a team of researchers and students from the Department of Robotics, Faculty of Information Science and Computing (FIC), at the University of Macau (UM).

This repository hosts our latest work and projects in the following areas:

* Learning-based control and optimal control
* Robot manipulation
* Robot Learning
* RL/ML and generative AI in robotics
* Embodied Intelligence
* Safety analysis for AI-driven robotic system
